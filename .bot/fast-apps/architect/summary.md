# fast-apps — architect's log

A new app is made fast. Test-speed's win (`origin/test-speed`, `.bot/test-speed/tester/test-speed.md`) is built clean on runtime2: about 75% of a test's CPU went to making a new app (§2), and on test-speed the C# sweep fell from 137–215 s to 29–31 s and `plang --test` from 24–31 s to 5.0–5.4 s (§21). Nothing merges from test-speed. It's the map and the measurements; the coder builds, the tester measures. This is the fast-apps half of the snapshot plan (`test/plan/snapshot/start.md`, "Frozen parts and children"), done first. Newest first; numbered from 1 (decisions are numbered per branch).
