# PLang — Brand Foundation (v2)

## Vision
**An internet made by the people who use it.**

## Promise
**Say what you need. Own what you get.**

## Belief
Today, software is made by a few companies and rented to everyone else. Your data lives on their servers, your identity is a password they hold, and every connection goes through a middleman.
PLang turns that around. People describe what they need, and it gets built. What they get runs on their own computer, with their data on their own disk. It connects to other people directly, with an identity and a signature instead of an account and a password.
The language underneath is plain English, so anyone who wants to can read exactly what an app does. Most people never will, and they don't need to.

## Pillars (what changes for a person)
| Pillar | Line | Fact underneath |
|---|---|---|
| **Describe it** | Say what you need. It gets built. | The LLM writes PLang goals at build time |
| **Yours** | It runs on your computer. Your data stays on your disk. | Apps are confined to their own folder; data lives on its own disk |
| **Identity** | No passwords. No accounts. | Every person and every app has a keypair |
| **Signed** | What you send arrives as you sent it. | All I/O is signed by default; tampered data is rejected |
| **Open** | Anyone can read what an app does. | Plain-language goals, plain JSON build output, no AI at runtime |

## Rules
- PLang the language is proof, not the pitch. It appears once, in the "Open" section, plus a short "for builders" section.
- Never mention PlangOS.
- Tone: calm conviction. A revolution, stated plainly. No hype words.
- White background with dark mode (dark grey, not black). The puffin is the one you talk to: the avatar beside the request.
- Builds compile on llm.plang.is (no API key). Install: `irm https://plang.is/start.ps1 | iex` (Windows 10/11); macOS/Linux "coming".
