Separator — what the string is split on, a `separator` · say: `by ","`, `on ";"`, `into lines`, `line by line` · ask: what does the step split on — a named separator (line, comma, tab, space, semicolon) or one of the step's variables? · builder: the separator the step names, as a quoted text — `into lines` / `line by line` → `Separator="line"`, `by tab` → `"tab"`, `by space` → `"space"`; a literal the step quotes stays as written (`on ";"` → `";"`); left out when the step names none (then the default comma).
Empty — what becomes of the empty pieces · say: `drop the empty ones` · builder: left out keeps them; `Empty="drop"` when the step drops them.
