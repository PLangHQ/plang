# Decisions

For someone who has read a rule in the [contributor guide](../contributor/rules.md) and asks why. Each record says what was chosen, why, what was rejected, and how it is held. The reason is taken from the repository only (`Documentation/v0.2/`, the source comments, `CLAUDE.md`). Where the repository does not write a reason down, the record says "reason not recorded". Nothing is inferred.

A record is the one place history may appear: a decision with its date and who made it, when the repository states them.
